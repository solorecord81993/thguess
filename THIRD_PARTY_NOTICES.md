# Location data

The 3,027 Thailand locations in `lib/thailand-pool.js` are adapted from
[WorldGuessr world-main.json](https://github.com/codergautam/worldguessr/blob/master/data/world-main.json), retrieved 2026-09-23.
Changes: Thailand-only filtering, compact fields, stable identifiers and geographic sampling buckets.

Street View imagery remains provided by Google; this dataset is coordinates, not imagery.
Panorama availability can change. Not every location has been visually verified in THGUESS.

Normal games remember selections in this browser until the pool is exhausted, then reuse the least-used locations.
Clearing browser storage, another device, or concurrent tabs can reset or diverge that history.
Daily challenges deliberately repeat during the same day. Bangkok retains its curated 16-location pool.
